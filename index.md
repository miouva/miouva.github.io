# 🌿 Miouva — a little world of gentle stationery

Welcome to Miouva.  
An independent European stationery brand inspired by soft colours, quiet afternoons and the beauty of slow living.

We create small paper treasures designed for journaling, planning and everyday creativity — each piece made to bring warmth, calmness and gentle inspiration to your day.

## ✨ Official Store
👉 https://miouva.com

## 💛 Our Stationery
- Soft-tone **notepads** for writing and everyday notes  
- **Planner refills** (A5, Personal, B5) for journaling and organisation  
- **PET tapes** with delicate illustrations  
- **Washi tapes**, stickers and vintage-style paper pieces  
- Accessories for notebooks, planners & scrapbooking  

## 🌱 Quality & Sustainability
All paper goods are printed in Europe using FSC-certified paper and eco-friendly production methods.
